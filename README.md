# Global Climate Trends Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes global trends in temperature change, CO₂ emissions, and sea level using Python. Our team cleaned, filtered, and visualized multiple datasets to compare climate patterns across 10 countries from 1993–2021.

The goal was to identify long-term trends, compare differences between countries, and explore how major climate indicators changed over time.

## Questions Explored
- How have temperature changes developed over time across selected countries?
- Which countries experienced the largest average temperature changes?
- How have CO₂ emissions changed across countries?
- Which countries contributed the highest levels of CO₂ emissions?
- How has global sea level changed over time?
- What similarities can be observed among these climate trends?

## Analysis Process
1. Imported climate datasets from multiple sources.
2. Filtered the data to selected countries and years.
3. Cleaned and reshaped the datasets for analysis.
4. Calculated summary statistics including averages and standard deviations.
5. Created bar charts, line charts, box plots, scatter plots, and area charts.
6. Compared trends across temperature, CO₂ emissions, and sea level.
7. Summarized the major findings from the analysis.

## Key Findings

### Temperature Change
- Germany had the highest average temperature change among the selected countries at approximately **1.32°C**.
- India had the lowest average temperature change at approximately **0.47°C**.
- Sweden showed the greatest variability in temperature change.
- The overall temperature-change measure increased from approximately **0.25°C in 1993 to 1.54°C in 2019**.

### CO₂ Emissions
- The United States had the highest average CO₂ emissions among the selected countries.
- Egypt had the lowest average CO₂ emissions.
- China showed the greatest variability in CO₂ emissions.
- CO₂ emissions generally increased over the study period, with the United States and China producing substantially higher emissions than the other selected countries.

### Global Sea Level
- Global sea level showed a clear upward trend over the period analyzed.
- The analyzed sea-level measure increased from approximately **-17.98 mm in 2000 to 50.91 mm in 2020**.
- The highest observed value was approximately **64.39 mm in 2019**.
